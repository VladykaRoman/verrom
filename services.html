Our Core Services

Verrom Limited provides a comprehensive suite of electrical solutions tailored for residential, commercial, and local authority projects throughout London and the Home Counties.

1. Residential Electrical Services

We ensure your home is safe, compliant, and powered for modern living.

    • Full & Partial Rewiring: Replacing outdated or unsafe wiring to meet current UK safety standards.
    • Consumer Unit Upgrades: Installing modern fuse boxes with RCD protection to prevent shocks and fires.
    • Lighting Design & Installation: Energy-efficient LED lighting, garden lighting, and smart lighting controls.
  • Extra Sockets & Circuits: Adding power points, USB sockets, and dedicated circuits for heavy appliances (ovens, showers).
  • Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging: Professional installation of home charging points for electric vehicles.

2. Commercial Electrical Services

We provide dependable, high-quality electrical solutions for businesses, offices, and retail spaces across London. Whether you are moving into a new office or need a reliable partner for building maintenance, Verrom Limited delivers on time and within budget.

  • Office & Retail Fit-Outs: Complete electrical design and installation for new business premises.
  • Power Distribution: Installation of heavy-duty power systems and additional sockets for workstations.
  • Energy-Efficient Lighting: LED upgrades to reduce your business energy bills and improve the workspace.
  • Safety Testing (EICR): Mandatory safety inspections and certifications to keep your business compliant with UK law.
  • Data Cabling: Professional networking and data point installation for your office IT systems.
  • Emergency Lighting & Fire Alarms: Keeping your staff and customers safe with essential safety system installations.

3. Safety, Testing & Compliance

We help landlords and business owners stay legal with official certification.

  • EICR (Electrical Installation Condition Reports): Mandatory 5-year safety checks for rental properties and businesses.
  • Emergency Lighting: Installation and annual testing to ensure safe evacuation routes during power failures.
  • Fire Alarm Systems: Professional installation and maintenance to comply with UK fire regulations.
  • PAT Testing: Routine safety checks for portable appliances in the workplace.
